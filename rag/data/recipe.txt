Recipe: Classic Margherita Pizza

Ingredients:
- 300g pizza dough
- 150g tomato sauce
- 200g fresh mozzarella
- 10 fresh basil leaves
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt to taste

Instructions:
1. Preheat the oven to 250°C (482°F).
2. Roll out the dough on a floured surface.
3. Spread the tomato sauce evenly, leaving a 2cm border.
4. Tear the mozzarella into pieces and distribute over the sauce.
5. Bake for 8-10 minutes until the crust is golden.
6. Remove from oven, add fresh basil and drizzle with olive oil.

Preparation time: 20 minutes
Cooking time: 10 minutes
Servings: 4
