The Lighthouse Keeper

Every evening, old Tomas climbed the spiral stairs of the lighthouse on Gray Point.
He had kept the light burning for thirty-one years, ever since the storm that took
his brother's fishing boat. The town below rarely thanked him, but he didn't mind.
One winter night, a small sailboat lost its way in thick fog. Tomas noticed the light
flickering unusually fast — a signal he didn't recognize. He climbed down, rowed out
himself, and guided the boat safely to shore. The sailor turned out to be his brother's
grandson, who had never known the lighthouse keeper was family.
